# **Tournament Director Setup**

Use this page to configure the tournament before play begins. Set the tournament name, starting stack, player count, buy-in, blind schedule, and payout structure, then launch the event.

## **General & Rules**

Enter:

- tournament name
- starting stack
- number of tables
- player name entry timing
- buy-in
- currency
- level length

The starting player count does not include players who may register late.

### **Late Registration**

Enable late registration if players may join the tournament after it has started. If this option is enabled, set the level at which late registration closes.

### **Tournament Type**

Choose from six tournament formats:

1. Freezeout
   - Players are eliminated permanently.
   - This is the default format.
2. Re-entry
   - Eliminated players may re-enter the tournament.
   - By default, the maximum number of re-entries is 1.
3. Rebuy
   - Players who are still in the tournament may rebuy chips.
   - By default, they receive a stack equal to their original starting stack for the cost of one buy-in.

   Allow add-on:
   - Players may buy an additional stack at a discounted rate.
   - By default, one buy-in buys 1.5x the original starting stack.
4. Bounty
   - Every player has a bounty on their head.
   - Busting a player pays out that bounty.
5. Mystery Bounty
   - Bust a player to reveal a bounty envelope.
   - The prize amount is determined by the envelope.

<Card title="Bounty Tournaments" type="note" horizontal>
  For a bounty tournament, enter the bounty amount for each player. For a mystery bounty tournament, configure the envelope values and quantities.
</Card>

## **Levels & Chips**

Set the tournament structure here.

Ante

- Choose the ante structure.
- By default, the game uses a big blind ante.

Chip denominations

- Select every chip denomination being used.
- This determines the default blind structure.

Blind schedule and break intervals

- A default 12-level schedule is provided.
- Adjust the schedule to match your tournament needs.

## **Players Roster**

Enter each player’s name in the roster. Do not include players who will register late. If you instead choose to enter player names later, do not enter anything.

## **Payout Structure**

The payout structure includes three columns:

- Places
- Pay type
- Amount

Places

- Enter the finish positions in the current pay bracket.
- You can use either single values, such as 5, or ranges, such as 5–9.

Pay type

- Choose between:
  - % of pool
  - Fixed amount

Amount

- Enter the payout for the selected bracket.
- If a payout bracket covers multiple places, the total prize money in that bracket is split evenly among the players in that range.

<Card title="Simultaneous Eliminations" type="note">
  Choose how players eliminated simultaneously should be ranked

  - Split Evenly: shared prize money is split equally among tied players.
  - Rank by Stack: tied players are ordered by chip stack, with larger stacks receiving higher payouts.
</Card>
