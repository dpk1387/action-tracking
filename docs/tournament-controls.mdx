# **Tournament Controls**

Use the tournament controls to manage blind timing, player entries, rebuys, payouts, and tournament flow while the event is in progress.

Pause / Resume Timer

- Pauses or resumes the blind timer.
- Use this when you want to stop the clock temporarily or continue the tournament.

Rewind / Forward 1 Minute

- Adds or subtracts 1 minute from the blind timer.
- Useful if the level needs to be adjusted mid-tourney.

Set Level

- Jumps to a selected level. Optionally, enter the time remaining in the level.
- You can also return to a prior level if needed.

Next Level

- Ends the current level early and starts the next one.

Late Registration

- Registers a player who joins after the tournament has started.
- This option is disabled once late registration has closed or is not available for the current format.

Re-entry

- Re-enters a player who has already been eliminated.
- The re-entry cost is added to the prize pool.

Rebuy

- Records a player’s rebuy.
- The rebuy amount is added to the prize pool.

Record Bust

- Marks the player or players who have just busted out.
- This is especially important once the tournament reaches the money.

Field -

- Reduces field size without selecting a specific player that was eliminated.
- This is useful when the money bubble is not yet relevant and you do not want to track exact bustouts by player.

<Note>
  The “-” button is disabled when only two players remain. This prevents accidental misclicks, since the tournament ends automatically when one player remains.
</Note>

Enter Names

Hand-for-Hand On / Off

- Turns hand-for-hand play on or off.
- When enabled, a notice appears under the timer indicating that hand-for-hand is active.

Structure Setup

- Edits the tournament structure, including blind levels and timing.

End Session

- Ends the tournament and gives you the option to save it for later.

Reset

- Restarts the tournament from its beginning.
