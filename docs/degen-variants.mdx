---
title: "Degen Variants"
---

Play a variety of degenerate No-Limit Texas Hold' Em variants!

**2nd Best**: 2nd Best hand at showdown wins the pot.

**3-2-1**: 3 flop cards, 2 turn cards, 1 river card.

**Ocean**: After river betting, the ocean (6th street) is dealt and one more betting round occurs before showdown.

**Naked Hold 'Em**: All mucked cards and burn cards are shown face up.

**Mandatory 3-Bet**: Flat calling and limping is banned preflop until a 3-bet occurs.

**Red River**: If the river is red, after the river betting round, another river is dealt and one more betting round occurs. This repeats until a river is black.

**Win the Button**: The winner of the previous hand gets the BTN position for the next hand.

**Deuces Wild**: All deuces are wild! A player dealt 22 must show immediately and will take the blinds and antes and a re-deal happens.
