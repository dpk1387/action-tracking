---
title: "GO Mode"
---

GO Mode is a deckless pass & play version of poker where one phone is passed around and your entire group can play NLH without a deck or a surface for cards!

Setup for a GO Mode game is the exact same as in Action Tracker mode, but with an additional **Stack Approximation** setting. Because there are no physical chips, Stack Approximation gives players an estimate of a player's stack. While Exact stack is provided as an option, we recommend you only give a good estimate of other stacks instead of the exact amount.

## Game Security

Because game integrity is very important, after a player takes their action and passes it to you, a screen verifying it is your action shows up to prevent misclicks.

## Game Controls

**Show stacks**: Show all other player's stacks, rounded based on your Exact Approximation setting.

**Show/hide cards**: Show or hide your hole cards.

**Switch Mode**: Switch to Action Tracker Mode (e.g. if you now have a space to play with a physical deck)

**History**: View the hand history. 

**End**: End the session, with the option to save or discard the session.

**Settings**: Change the visual settings of the game.
